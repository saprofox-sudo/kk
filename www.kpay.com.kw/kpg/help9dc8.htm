What is a ATM PIN?
Personal Identification Number (PIN) is the secret numeric password used to authenticate the user while performing any ATM, Point of sale and internet transactions. The Cardholder expresses authority to the Bank for carrying out transactions and instructions authenticated by the PIN. You will be required to enter your PIN only once for every transaction.
What is a ECommerce PIN ?
ECommerce PIN is the secret alpha numeric special characters password used to authenticate the user while performing any internet transactions. Customer should have registered for ECommerce PIN through ATM/Internet. Customer has to change the ecommerce PIN before doing a online transaction for the first time. The Cardholder expresses authority to the Bank for carrying out transactions and instructions authenticated by the ECommerce PIN. You will be required to enter your Ecommerce PIN only once for every transaction.
